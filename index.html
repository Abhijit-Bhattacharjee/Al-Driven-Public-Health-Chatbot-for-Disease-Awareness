Problem analysis

Healthcare information must reach people before illness becomes severe.

01

Access gap

Rural and semi-urban communities may have limited nearby doctors, long travel time, and low access to reliable health information.

02

Language gap

People need answers in familiar local language, with simple words and clear next steps instead of complex medical terms.

03

Speed gap

Outbreak warnings, vaccine reminders, and prevention advice lose value when they arrive late or through channels people do not use.

How it works

From a simple message to trusted public health guidance.

1

Citizen asks

Users send questions through WhatsApp, SMS, or a web chat interface in their preferred language.

2

NLP understands

Rasa or Dialogflow detects intent, symptoms, age group, location, and urgency level from natural language.

3

Health data syncs

The backend checks approved health content, vaccination rules, local facilities, and outbreak alert feeds.

4

Action is sent

The chatbot replies with prevention steps, schedule reminders, and escalation advice when danger signs appear.

Technical feasibility

Cloud-ready architecture for scale, safety, and measurable accuracy.

The system can be built as a modular service: NLP handles intent detection, a verified knowledge layer controls medical content, and messaging APIs distribute responses through web, WhatsApp, and SMS.

NLP layer

Rasa, Dialogflow, multilingual intent models, entity extraction, confidence scoring.

Integration layer

Government health APIs, FHIR-ready records, facility lookup, outbreak alert feeds.

Delivery layer

WhatsApp Business API, SMS gateway, responsive web app, low-bandwidth message design.

Cloud layer

Container deployment, autoscaling, analytics dashboard, audit logs, content approval workflow.
